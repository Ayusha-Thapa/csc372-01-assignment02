Yule Ball

Date: December 18, 2026

Time: 7:00 PM–12:00 AM (midnight)

Location: Great Hall

Step into the beautifully decorated Great Hall for an unforgettable evening at the Hogwarts University Yule Ball. Dress in your finest robes and join students from all four houses for dancing, live music, and festive refreshments. Whether you arrive with friends or a dance partner, enjoy a magical night celebrating the season together.

A Night of Magic and Celebration

Celebrate the end of the semester with an enchanting evening in the Great Hall. Enjoy sparkling winter decorations, live music, and festive refreshments while spending time with friends from all four houses.

What to Expect

Come with friends, bring a dance partner, or attend on your own. Everyone is welcome to join the celebration!

  • Live music and dancing throughout the evening.
  • Festive snacks and drinks.
  • A winter-themed photo area for pictures with friends.
  • Formal robes or other comfortable formal clothing.

Evening Schedule

  • 7:00 PM: Arrival and welcome refreshments.
  • 7:30 PM: Opening dance in the Great Hall.
  • 8:00 PM: Live music and open dancing.
  • 10:00 PM: Dessert and photo break.
  • 11:30 PM: Final dances.
  • 12:00 AM: Event ends.

Accessibility and Participation

Step-free access to the Great Hall and seating near the dance floor will be available. Dancing is optional, and guests are welcome to relax and enjoy the music. Please contact the organizer before the event to discuss accessibility needs or dietary requirements.
