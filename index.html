About the Campus Event Guide

The Hogwarts University Office of Student Engagement helps students discover life beyond the classroom. Our Campus Event Guide brings together opportunities to meet friends, learn new skills, and celebrate university traditions. With event details in one place, you can easily plan your week and find activities that match your interests, from hands-on workshops to exciting house competitions.

Whether you enjoy cheering at Quidditch matches, creating potions, or dancing at the Yule Ball, there is a place for you in our campus community. We encourage students from all four houses to try something new, support one another, and build lasting friendships. Every event offers a chance to take a break from studying and make your time at Hogwarts a little more memorable.
